    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours, in six stages. The story first, the one missable trophy early, then robberies, collectibles and cleanup. Almost everything stays open after the credits — there is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed outright. Everything else stays available after the story — but a few strands are time-sensitive, so the notes below say exactly when to act.
